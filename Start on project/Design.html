What you should and shouldn't do.

What you should do is cook it to an appropriate temperature and ensure it is cooked evenly or long enough to be safe for consumption. Make sure to use a meat thermometer to check the internal temperature. Make sure to wash your hands before and after handling raw meat.

What you shouldn't do is consume the meat if it has been left out at room temperature for more than two hours. Also, avoid cross-contamination by using separate cutting boards and utensils for raw and cooked meat. For the chicken never wash the chicken before cooking it will spread bacteria everywhere.

You can also use malum as the sauce for the meat to give it some sweet flavor by blending the apples and then put it on top. You can also use some herbs and spices to give it a more savory flavor most commonly used herbs and spices are black pepper, salt, garlic. You also can use olive mainly for chicken and steak. You can add fat by adding butter, milk or oil to the meat. Make sure wherever you are cooking the meat on a pan and if there are juice on the pan, make sure to use it as a sauce for the meat aswell. Recommended use a non stick pan for cooking in case the meat sticks to the pan though you don't have to use one all the time.

Minimum requirement for cooking each type of meat.

For most meats you have to cook them all the way through to ensure they are safe to eat. However, there is one exception which is steak which can be cooked at a lower temperature or you can cook it as rare as blue rare. This is because most of the harmful bacteria are at the surface of the meat for steak unless the meat is minced then you have to cook it all the way through.
