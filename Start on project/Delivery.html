Delivery Information

How does delivery work?

Our delivery sevice works by having the customers order online and then we will deliver the meat to your house. The meat will be delivered in a coolant box to make sure that the meat is kept at a cold temperature. This would slow down the growth of bacteria keep the meat fresh for longer. The meat will be delivered on the next day next to the house or at the front door.

What is the minimum mass for delivery?

Depending on the type of meat, the minimum mass for delivery can vary because it would be counted in slices where one slice of meat would average 100g. Though the the minimum mass for delivery could be higher for some type of meat. However, for milk and eggs, the minimum amount for delivery is 1 liter for milk and 6 for eggs. Some of the meats can be delivered in shorter pieces like chicken, you can order either the breast, leg or the whole chicken.

What is the maximum amount for delivery?

The maximum amount for delivery can vary depending on the meat. For example, some of the meats can only be delivered in small amounts because of the price and the size of the meat. And some of the meat can be delivered in larger amounts because of the price and the size of the meat. And it some of the meat are ligher than others. Also some of the meat can only be delivered in small amounts in this season compared to this season because there are less of them in some seasons.
